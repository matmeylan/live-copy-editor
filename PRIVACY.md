# Privacy Policy — Live Copy Editor

_Last updated: 2026-09-25_

Live Copy Editor does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **What is stored.** The text edits and comments you make, the page URL and title they belong to, and the current mode (Off, Edit or Comment).
- **Where it is stored.** Only in your browser, in the extension's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). Nothing is sent to any server, including ours. We don't run one.
- **Who can see it.** Only you, on the browser profile where you made the edits.
- **How to delete it.** Click **Clear** in the side panel, or remove the extension.
- **Clipboard.** When you click **Export prompt**, the prompt is written to your clipboard. The extension never reads your clipboard.
- **Page content.** The extension reads the text of elements you click in order to log your changes. It does not read, record or transmit anything else from the pages you visit.

No analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/matmeylan/live-copy-editor-/issues
